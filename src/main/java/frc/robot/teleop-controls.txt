========= controls ===========

right trigger ..... feed shooter
right bumper ...... agitate
left trigger ...... intake
left bumper ....... outtake
X ................. autotarget
A ................. shoot distance override (set distance to low)
options ........... reset imu
B ................. deploy intake
Y ................. retract intake

hood will be down UNLESS feeding or autotargeting or shoot distance override (X or right trigger or A)

up ................ hood offset up
down .............. hood offset down
right ............. shoot offset up
left .............. shoot offset down
